Welcome to Luanshya Muncipal Council

Luanshya District is located on the Copperbelt Province of Zambia. The District is 337 kilometers from the country’s capital city, Lusaka and 35 kilometers from Ndola, the Provincial headquarters of the Copperbelt Province. Luanshya lies on an approximate latitude of 130 7’59.880” s and longitude 280 23’ 60.00”. Additionally, it has an altitude of 1257metres above sea level and boasts of an area of 1, 007.6087 km2. Furthermore, the District has two (2) constituencies and thirty (30) wards.
